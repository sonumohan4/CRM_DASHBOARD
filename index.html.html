Deal value by stage

Definitions: revenue and orders count deals marked Won, at their estimated deal value. Customers are all companies in the sheet; active means any status except Lost. Lead conversion is Won deals divided by all leads. Follow-ups are open deals (not Won or Lost) with a next follow-up date. Bulk deals are the largest estimated deal values; change the size on the pipeline board. Dates are measured from 20 Oct 2026. New follow-up dates you pick are saved in this browser only.
